How this works

The story is a prompt built from the world card (plot, characters, style) plus the recent turns. With the in-browser engine, the model weights download once from the MLC CDN and are cached by your browser, then run on your GPU through WebGPU. Nothing you type leaves your machine. First load is 1 to 3 GB depending on the model. Chrome, Edge, and recent Safari and Firefox on a desktop GPU work best. Your story is saved in this browser only.
